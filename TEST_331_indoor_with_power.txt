WHAT TO NOTE DURING THE RIDE
---------------------------------------------------------------------

The UPDATE PATTERN is as unexplained as the value. On both outdoor
rides it showed a small value early, then sat still for the whole
workout, then jumped at the completion signal. Checking it at these
points is the most informative thing you can collect:

  [ ] 0. BEFORE starting -- confirm it reads 00:00
  [ ] 1. power is actually being read -- the target gauge responding
         confirms the pedals are paired. If power is not live, the
         test is void and worth aborting early.
  [ ] 2. end of the warm-up, before interval 1
  [ ] 3. immediately after interval 1
  [ ] 4. immediately after interval 2   <- does it tick per interval?
  [ ] 5. after interval 4 (halfway)
  [ ] 6. after interval 7, before the cool-down
  [ ] 7. the moment the workout signals COMPLETE   <- the jump point
  [ ] 8. a few minutes into the cool-down
  [ ] 9. the final value when you stop

Steps 3 and 4 together answer a question nothing has: whether it
updates per interval or only once at the end. If it moves after
interval 1 and again after interval 2, it is accumulating and the
outdoor "it never changed" was an artefact of 10-second sprints being
too small to see.
